# Giggle 64: The Gauntlet of Fire

**A third-person action-adventure in the style of a late-90s 64-bit classic.**
Sword and shield, lock-on combat, towers and tombs, a horse named Biscuit, and a kingdom on fire.

## The story

> *The Gauntlet of Fire has been taken.*

Long ago, the Cosmic Deity **Lothelye** dreamt the world of Bunsriel, and three goddesses shaped it. When the
Dark Lord rose from the hells, a nameless warrior struck him down with the **Magical Sword of Bun**, and a weapon
that could burn the world was sealed away in Serielye's ice.

Now **Firebuns III**, heir of the Evil King, has walked into the frozen forge and taken the Gauntlet for himself.
In the quiet village of Haven, a page from the castle is knocking on the door of a knight who sleeps in a bit too
late: **Sir Gigglebuns IV**.

Climb the Goddess Towers, gather the pieces of the goddesses' Bun, draw the legendary Bun Sword, and see the war for
Bunsriel through to its end, across five chapters.

## Features

- **A whole kingdom to explore.** Bunsriel Fields and Haven Village at the center, with Mirror Lake, the Southern
  Fields, the deserts of Ladhellin, the snows of Frostpeak, the marshes, the Batela Fells and more around them.
  Everything is joined by loading roads, with a day and night cycle and changing weather.
- **More than fifteen dungeons.** These include the multi-floor Goddess Towers, a thirty-room castle and the
  forty-room Astral Rift. Each dungeon has its own keys, map, compass, item and master.
- **Combat that feels right.** Lock-on, side hops, back flips, jump slashes and shield blocks. The swing you get
  depends on how you move and what you're locked on to. Later, knights teach you new techniques, including the Great
  Spin, the Down Thrust, the Shield Bash and the Parry.
- **Twenty-eight items.** The Bow, the Boomerang and the Grapple Hook, plus bombs, the Bloom Rod, the Iron Flail and
  goddess magic. On top of that there are five swords, four shields, tunics and boots.
- **Life in the kingdom.** Ride Biscuit across the plains and fish every lake. Catch fairies in bottles, follow a
  trading chain and find the Goddess Springs. Hunt down 28 Heart Shards and explore the houses of Castle Town, Haven
  and Ashwick, where the townsfolk talk about whatever is happening in the story.
- **A journal that remembers.** Every quest is written down step by step, and people in town repeat the talk of the
  day if you lose your way.
- **The real 64-bit look.** Native 320 × 240 rendering, bilinear-filtered textures and an optional **TRUE N64 MODE**,
  in 4:3 or widescreen.
- **Online co-op for up to six.** Play as Sir Gigglebuns, Pink, Aladriel, Idrith, Mira or Himeko. Each has the hero's
  full moveset and her own take on every sword, shield and tunic. The party shares one world: items, coins and progress
  are shared, everyone fights the same creatures, and the party travels between areas together. PvP is optional. Host
  or join from **OPTIONS › ONLINE**.
- **Three save files.** OoT-style Game Over with Continue, rumble, remappable controls, and Xbox, PlayStation or
  Nintendo button icons.

## Controls

The game is made for an Xbox-style controller, but it plays fine on a keyboard and mouse. Any button can be remapped
in **OPTIONS › CONTROLS**.

| | Controller | Keyboard / mouse |
|---|---|---|
| Move | Left stick | WASD (Ctrl to walk) |
| Action: talk, open, roll, climb, dive | **A** | Space / E |
| Sword | **B** | Left click / J |
| Items | **X** / **Y** | 1 / 2 |
| Shield | **RT** | Right click / K |
| Lock-on | **LT** | Q / middle click |
| First-person view | **RB** | F |
| Camera behind you | **LB** | Shift |
| Pause screen | **Menu** | Esc |
| Minimap | **View** | M |

The [instruction booklet](promo/manual/giggle64_manual.pdf) explains everything else.

## Running from source

1. Install **[Godot 4.7.2](https://godotengine.org/download/archive/)** (standard build, not .NET).
2. Clone this repository, open Godot's Project Manager, choose **Import** and select `project.godot`.
   The first import takes a few minutes.
3. Press **F5**.

Optional launch flags go after `--`:

| Flag | What it does |
|---|---|
| `--skip-title` | Start straight in the game |
| `--skip-splash` | Go straight to the title screen |
| `--fps=21` | Run at 21, 30 or 60 frames per second |
| `--net-host[=port]` | Host an online game (port 24064 by default) |
| `--net-join=address[:port]` | Join someone's online game |
| `--net-char=pink` | Play as `gigglebuns`, `pink`, `aladriel`, `idrith`, `mira` or `himeko` |
| `--net-name=NAME` | The name over your head online |

### For developers

- **Generated assets.** Models, animations, textures, sounds and the world are built by the generators in
  [`tools/`](tools). Edit the generators, not the generated `.tscn` and `.tres` files, then rebuild with
  `tools/build_assets.ps1 -Godot <path to Godot>`. Sound effects are recordings (listed in
  [`tools/recorded_sfx.txt`](tools/recorded_sfx.txt)), which `tools/gen_sfx.gd` leaves alone.
- **Tests.** Run `Godot --headless --path . --fixed-fps 60 res://tests/test_runner.tscn` (add `-- --tests=a,b` for a
  subset). Visual capture scenes and the geometry audit are described in [`tests/README.md`](tests/README.md).
- **Debug menu.** Press **F1** (or **View + Menu**) in a development build.
- **Reporting a bug.** Press **F8** in game. It saves a bug report (a screenshot, where you are, your file's state and
  the recent log) to the game's `bug_reports` folder. Send that folder along with a sentence about what happened.
- **Design documents.** The plan for the whole saga is in [`design/masterplan.txt`](design/masterplan.txt), and
  [`docs/technical_notes.md`](docs/technical_notes.md) has notes on how the movement, camera and other systems were
  built.

```
assets/      models, textures, music, sounds, fonts (mostly generated)
design/      story, world and item plans
docs/        technical notes
patch_notes/ one file per version
promo/       box art, cards, trailer, booklet, posters, world map
scenes/      the main scene, areas, enemies (areas are generated)
scripts/     the game: player, camera, combat, enemies, NPCs, story, UI
tests/       automated tests, capture scenes, geometry audit
tools/       asset and world generators, promo renderers
```
