# Giggle 64 — Alpha 1.2.0: Wide Open Fields

A widescreen option, and a livelier title screen: Sir Gigglebuns now goes for
a walk behind the logo, and a short scene plays when the title music ends.

## New option: 16:9 widescreen

- **Pause > OPTIONS > GRAPHICS > ASPECT RATIO** switches between **4:3** (the
  N64's shape, still the default) and **16:9**.
- In 16:9 you see more of the world at the sides. Nothing is stretched, and
  the view keeps the same height.
- The HUD moves out to the edges of the screen: hearts and Coins to the left,
  the buttons and the minimap to the right. Menus, text boxes and the title
  stay in the middle.
- Works with every RESOLUTION setting (720p 16:9 is 1280x720, 1080p is
  1920x1080, and so on).
- In a window, the window changes shape to fit (it keeps its height). The
  setting is saved with your other options.

## Title screen

- **Sir Gigglebuns walks through Haven** behind the logo: Haven Village,
  Bunsriel Fields, Old Castle Town, Mirror Lake, the Forest, the Desert of
  Ladhellin, the Southern Fields and Frostpeak, each filmed with its own
  slow camera.
- Townsfolk in the background now move as they idle. Monsters are kept out
  of the shots.
- **A new scene plays when the title music ends**: a bit of sword practice
  in Bunsriel Fields that goes wrong. It isn't part of the story. Press any
  button to skip it and go back to the title. Once it finishes, the screen
  fades back to the splash screens and the title.
- Before this update, the title waited 15 seconds after the music ended and
  then went back to the splashes. Now the scene starts 2 seconds after the
  music ends (12 seconds if you just backed out of the file select).
