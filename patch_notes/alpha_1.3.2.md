# Giggle 64 — Alpha 1.3.2: Songs of Haven

Every region and tower now has a music slot of its own. (The new themes are
written as MIDI and get added to the game once they are recorded.)

## Changes

- **New region themes:** Haven Village, Old Castle Town, The Forest, The Hidden
  Forest, the West Marshlands, the Southern Fields, the Northeastern Cliffs
  and Frostpeak each have their own track. Until a recording is added,
  these areas stay quiet, as before.
- **Ladhellin Tower, Skyreach Spire and the Bog Tower** have their own
  dungeon themes.
- Each theme is built on the main Giggle 64 melody, moved into the mood of
  its region. In the Bog Tower, the melody sounds warped until the Bloom
  section, where it sounds whole again.
