# Giggle 64 — Alpha 1.7.0: The Goddess Springs

The goddesses' blessings finally have a home. Five hidden springs are now in
the game, Sir Gigglebuns has a house of his own, and Haven Village has a
princess. (She insists.)

## The Goddess Springs

- **Five hidden springs**, after Ocarina of Time's Great Fairy Fountains. Stand
  on the glowing mark before the pool and press A to **PRAY**: the spring's
  keeper rises from the water.
  - **Alan's Spring** (Hidden Forest): behind a cracked boulder in the east
    cliff. Bomb it. Gives **Alan's Harvest**.
  - **Aphiel's Spring** (Northeastern Cliffs): a cave halfway up the
    mountain, on the third tier. Gives **Aphiel's Gale**.
  - **Serielye's Spring** (Desert of Ladhellin): in a heap of dark rock under
    the natural arch. Gives **Serielye's Ruin**.
  - **A Spring of Lothelye** (West Marshlands): on a rocky islet in a deep
    pool. Take the new boardwalk spur and Grapple Hook across. **Doubles your
    magic meter.**
  - **A Spring of Lothelye** (Southern Fields): behind a cracked boulder in
    the north-east hills. **Doubles your defense.**
- Every return visit, the keeper fills your hearts and magic.
- Fairies drift over every spring's pool. Bring a bottle.
- The first blessing you receive brings the magic meter with it.

## Home

- **A new game now begins in Sir Gigglebuns' house.** He wakes up in bed, the
  rooster crows, and he stretches and hops out (skippable once seen).
- His house has a hearth, a table with a basket of buns, his journal on the
  desk, spare gear by the door, and portraits of Gigglebuns I, II and III.
  The door leads out onto his mound in Haven Village.

## Himeko, Princess of Haven Village

- A new villager: **Himeko**, who calls herself the Princess of Haven Village.
  She is not one. She wears a crimson furisode, has long black hair in a hime
  cut, and carries a fan. "Ohohoho!"
- Her pink **palace** stands on the east lane, with turrets, a rose garden and
  a red carpet up to the door. Inside: a giant portrait of herself, a gilded
  chair, a chandelier, a canopy bed, a tea table, a vanity and an empty pond.
- She appoints you her personal knight and asks for a **fish for her pond**.
  Bring one in a bottle for a reward.

## Changes

- **Fish in a bottle:** when you land a fish with an empty bottle on you, you
  can keep it in the bottle. Let it out by water and it swims away.
- **The magic meter is now blue**, and so are the magic jars and the magic
  cost gems on the pause screen.
- **Double defense:** halves the damage of every blow, and your hearts get a
  white rim.
- **The EQUIPMENT page** now matches SELECT ITEM: trays with name plates for
  Swords, Shields and Boots, the hero in a tray of his own with a note on the
  piece under the cursor, and a new **UPGRADE** tray showing your quiver,
  bomb bag, nut pouch, wallet, magic meter and defense with their sizes.
