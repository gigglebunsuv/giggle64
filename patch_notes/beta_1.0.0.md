# Giggle 64 — Beta 1.0.0: The Gauntlet's Trail

Giggle 64 is now in **Beta**! The story continues past the lost battle with
Chapter II, two new dungeons, new clothes, new games to play around Haven, and
a careful pass over saves, controls and performance. Old saves carry over.

## Chapter II: The Gauntlet's Trail

- **The morning after.** After the ending, the hero wakes at home three days
  later, and Princess Himeko bursts in with the news.
- **Castle Town's survivors** now shelter in Himeko's palace: Pink, the
  Questlords Aladriel and Idrith, and Tansy. Himeko runs the place, badly and
  very proudly. Walk in to hear where Firebuns III went.
- **The Iron Mallet** waits in the castle armory on the ground floor, too
  heavy for the goblins to carry off.
- **A new quest chain:** the Iron Mallet → Frostpeak Hollow → the Bun Bracers →
  the Sun Trial → the Gauntlet's trail. It ends, for now, with a clue to where
  the Sapphire Order is going.
- **Haven after the fall:** on Bunsriel Fields and the South Road, Sapphire
  Order camps (a mage and two goblin sentries round a blue fire) appear at
  night, and Haven's people have new things to say.

## New dungeon: Frostpeak Hollow

- Behind the frozen falls on Frostpeak's east cliff. Smash the ice with the
  **Iron Mallet** to get in.
- Three storeys of blue ice: stakes to drive in, a channel of freezing water to
  cross on frozen floes, and a false wall for the Looking Glass.
- **The cold bites.** Inside, a countdown runs on the HUD; when it runs out you
  lose hearts. The **Frost Mantle** keeps the cold off.
- Mini-boss **Rimeguard, the Frozen Sentry** guards the **Frost Arrows**.
- Boss **Sleetcoil, the Meltwater Serpent**: its scales turn the sword, but a
  Frost Arrow into it while it rears freezes it solid.

## New dungeon: the Sun Trial

- At the foot of the great mesa in the south-east of the Desert of Ladhellin,
  sealed by two heavy stones that only the **Bun Bracers** can lift.
- An open court of sunlight and mirrors, a dark room of shades, and
  **the Sunwarden**, who guards the **Sun Arrows**.
- The sanctum's last door opens to a sun switch too high for any mirror. Use a
  Sun Arrow.

## Tunics and the Knight's Regalia

- A new **TUNICS** row on the EQUIPMENT page:
  - **Haven Tunic**: the red one.
  - **Tide Tunic**: stay underwater as long as you like (hold A while diving).
    It's in a sunken chest seven meters down in Mirror Lake.
  - **Frost Mantle**: keeps out the cold of Frostpeak Hollow. Sold by
    **Hilde**, the new keeper of the mountain hut on Frostpeak.
  - **Shade Cloak**: enemies notice you only from much closer. A gift from
    Idrith.
- **The Knight's Regalia**, from Himeko: she has always thought her knight was
  poorly dressed. Bring her the fish, then visit her again. It's a vanity
  outfit of ivory and crimson with gold trim and a cape that streams behind
  you. Wear it over any tunic, and the tunic's power still works.

## Upgrades

- **Bun Bracers**: lift heavy rocks. Aladriel gives them to you after
  Frostpeak Hollow. Heavy rocks hide a Heart Shard in the north-west hills
  of Bunsriel Fields, a chest in the West Marshlands, and coins out on the
  field.
- **Silver and Golden Scales**: dive twice as deep, then deeper still. Mira
  gives them for a 12-pound and a 14-pound fish. The lake's true lord (14 or
  15 pounds) now turns up now and then. A trench at the bottom of Mirror Lake
  hides a Heart Shard.
- **Biggest Quiver (50)**: from **Clover's Target Range** by the crossroads in
  the Southern Fields. Hit every gold center in 45 seconds.
- **Giant Wallet (500)**: from **Tansy's Cups** (the shell game). Win five rounds
  in a row. She sets up by the Castle Town fountain, and after the fall in
  Himeko's palace.

## The Journal

- A sixth pause page, the **JOURNAL**, is written like the hero's own journal.
  It lists the main story chapter by chapter and every errand Haven's people
  give you, each step ticked off as you go.

## Little things

- **File select**: the highlighted file shows a completion panel with an
  overall percent and your items, Goddess Springs and Heart Shards, plus a
  CHAPTER II mark once the game is beaten.
- **Lord Bubbles** has a gilt plaque on Himeko's pond. Read it.
- **The portraits** of Gigglebuns I, II and III in the hero's house each have
  a story when you CHECK them, and a little more once you know the legend.
- **The rooster** of Haven Village pecks about near the hero's house. Chase
  him if you like. Hit him and... well.
- **Fish species**: Mirror Perch, Haven Carp, Lake Lord, Snowmelt Trout, Bog
  Catfish and Frostfin, each with its own record. Mira pins an outline of
  every kind you land on her **record board**.
- **Looking Glass secrets**: false walls hiding chests in all three Goddess
  Towers and in Frostpeak Hollow.
- **Title screen**: a red **BETA** stamp, and a new attract scene: Firebuns
  III gives his orders in fallen Castle Town while Pink watches.

## Changes

- **Saves are safer.** The game writes to a temporary file first and keeps the
  previous save as a backup; a damaged save falls back to its backup. Old alpha
  saves are upgraded when loaded, and the original file is kept. A save from a
  newer version of the game is left untouched.
- **OPTIONS > CONTROLS**: rebind A, B, X, Y, shield, target, look, camera,
  walk, minimap and START, for keyboard and gamepad. Binding a button that is
  already in use swaps the two. **RESET DEFAULTS** puts everything back.
- **Performance**: torch and fire lights now switch off when they're far from
  the hero or more than a storey above or below him. The castle and the towers
  light far fewer lights at once.
- The EQUIPMENT page has five trays now: swords, shields, tunics, boots and
  upgrades (with bracers and scales added).
- Mira hands out her fishing prizes even after the castle falls.
- **OPTIONS > CREDITS** rolls the credits (updated for the Beta) any time.
- Ottilie's Lantern can no longer be missed: she gives it on any later visit,
  even after the fall. Mira and Ottilie have new things to say in Chapter II.

## Debug

- The debug menu's ITEMS tab covers tunics, the outfit, bracers and scales;
  STATE covers Chapter II's flags and the new dungeons; SCENES can play
  Chapter II's two new scenes.
