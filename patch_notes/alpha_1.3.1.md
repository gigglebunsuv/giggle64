# Giggle 64 — Alpha 1.3.1: Sure Footing

A bug-fix pass on the dungeons and the horse.

## Fixes

- **You can now get into the Bog Tower.** The broken boardwalk in the West
  Marshlands now runs further out, so the mooring post's Grapple Hook target is
  within the chain's reach. The tower's islet is also bigger, so the Grapple Hook
  lands you on dry ground instead of pulling you into the moat.
- **Enemies stay in their own rooms.** A creature now only notices and
  chases you when you are in the same room as it. Before, enemies could see
  you through floors and walls. In the Bog Tower, for example, the Mire Knight
  in the cellar followed you around while you walked through the hall above it.
- **Rooms always clear.** When you beat the last enemy in a room, the room
  now counts as cleared even if you are standing outside it. Before, its chest
  never appeared and its doors stayed shut. This fixes the Bloom Rod chest in
  the Bog Tower, which could fail to appear after the Mire Knight was beaten.
- **Southern Tower:** a large cog wheel was hanging across the mill room's
  north doorway and hid the way out. It has been replaced by two smaller cogs,
  one on each side of the door.
- **Castle:** one of the kitchen hearths stood right in front of the cracked
  wall to the pantry, so you couldn't get through even after bombing the wall.
  The hearth has been moved.

## Changes

- **Riding Biscuit now matches Epona in Ocarina of Time:**
  - The stick steers her from her own point of view. Up means keep going,
    and left or right turns her, no matter where the camera is pointing.
  - Pushing the stick to the side while she is standing turns her on the
    spot.
  - Pulling the stick back while she is standing makes her walk backward.
    Push it left or right at the same time to steer as she backs up.
  - Pulling back at a gallop makes her dig in and skid to a stop.
  - When you start from a standstill, she pauses for a moment before she
    walks off. If you let go of the stick while walking, she stops at the
    end of her stride.
  - You can only get off when she is standing still. You can't spur her on
    while she is turning on the spot or backing up.
