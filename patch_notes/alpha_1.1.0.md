# Giggle 64 — Alpha 1.1.0: The Fall of Castle Town

The story reaches its end (for now). Draw the Bun Sword and Firebuns III takes
Old Castle Town with the Gauntlet of Fire. Fight your way up the burning
Castle of Haven to the throne room for the last battle.

## New dungeon: the Bog Tower

- The sealed tower in the West Marshlands is open. Grapple Hook across to its
  islet and go in.
- 10 rooms over four storeys: the Root Hall, a flooded cellar, sinking sludge,
  a pond of deadly bog and the roof.
- New treasure: **the Bloom Rod**. Aim it like the Grapple Hook and fling a seed:
  - on ground it bursts into a **bloom-pod** you can climb, weigh down a
    switch with, or shoot the Grapple Hook into;
  - on water or sludge it opens into a **lily pad** you can stand on;
  - only one bloom grows at a time.
- New enemies: **Bog Skeletons** and the **Mire Knight** mini-boss.
- Boss: **Gullagog, the Mire Toad**. It leaps (mind the shockwave), lashes its
  tongue (your shield stops it) and inhales. Throw a seed or a bomb into its
  mouth while it inhales and it chokes, flips over and leaves its belly open.
- One Heart Shard, behind a wall only a bomb can open.

## New item: Bombs

- **Marigold's Goods** sells a **Bomb Bag** (80 Coins, 20 bombs) and
  **Bombs (10)** (25 Coins).
- Press the bombs' button to pull one out, lit. Throw it with A, B or an item
  button, or stand still and press A to set it down. It goes off after
  3.5 seconds (the same fuse as OoT) and hurts everything nearby, you too.
- Bombs open cracked walls and blow up the cracked boulders blocking caves
  around Haven. Behind them are prizes, including **three more Pieces of
  Heart**.
- Once you own the bag, bombs drop from pots, grass and enemies.

## The story: the fall of Castle Town

- Before you draw the Bun Sword, the stone now warns you: **there is no going
  back**. Pink's Armory and Marigold's Goods close for good afterward. You
  can answer "Not yet".
- **New cutscene:** Firebuns III and the Sapphire Order march into Old Castle
  Town, he raises the Gauntlet of Fire, and the town and castle go up in
  flames.
- **Old Castle Town has fallen:** scorched streets, fires, goblins and
  Sapphire mages. The armory is barred and the castle gate is torn open.
- Mira, Ottilie and Clover have new things to say.

## Final dungeon: the Castle of Haven

- The biggest dungeon yet: **30 rooms over seven storeys**, from the
  dungeons up to the throne room under a burning sky.
- It uses everything: bombs, the Bloom Rod with the Grapple Hook, the Mirror
  Shield's sunbeams, fire arrows, the Wind Boots in a gale on the ramparts,
  and more.
- New enemies: **Sapphire Mages** (they vanish and reappear, and their bolts
  can be reflected), **Sapphire Knights**, **charred Skeletons** and **fire
  cave bat**. Mini-bosses: the **Sapphire Warden** and the **Cinder Wyvern**.
- **New cutscene:** find Pink, Aladriel and Idrith locked in the dungeons.
  Pink hands you the castle's Boss Key and Idrith shares a honey bun.
- **The last battle against Firebuns III**, and how it ends. Stay for the
  ending and the credits. Afterward you can save and carry on from Haven
  Village.

## Cutscenes and boss entrances

- A new cutscene system, after OoT's: letterbox bars, the HUD hides, a scene
  camera, and speaker names on the text boxes.
- **Boss entrances:** the first time you meet each boss (Carapax, Corvax,
  Kherash, Aeolos, Gullagog and Firebuns III), the camera sweeps from behind
  you to the boss as it rises and roars and its name appears, as in OoT's
  Gohma fight. After that, the fight starts straight away.

## All new sound effects

- Every sound effect, footstep and ambience has been redone. The old chip-tune
  bleeps are gone. Hits, swings, shields, pots, doors, water, wind, fire,
  voices and creatures are now made from realistic sound models, with a bit
  of room echo.
- Jingles (item get, secrets, heart vessels) are now played on harp, soft
  brass and bells.
- **Every sound is leveled to the same loudness**, so nothing blasts and
  nothing is lost.
- New ambiences for the Bog Tower and the ruins of Castle Town.

## Options

- **BUTTON ICONS** (GRAPHICS): prompts show the right buttons for your
  controller: Xbox (A B X Y in their colors), PlayStation (cross, circle,
  square, triangle) or Nintendo (with A and B in Nintendo's places). AUTO
  detects your pad, or you can choose one. Keyboard players see key names.
- **HUD STYLE** (GRAPHICS): CLASSIC (the OoT buttons) or MODERN (the four face
  buttons in a diamond, with your controller's icons).
- **RESOLUTION** (GRAPHICS): 240p (the N64's, default), 480p, 720p, 1080p,
  1440p or 4K. Higher settings draw the world sharper, with smoothed edges.
  The HUD and menus keep their layout.

## Fixes

- The hero no longer counts as "fallen out of the world" on dungeon floors
  more than 12 m below ground (needed for the castle's dungeons).
