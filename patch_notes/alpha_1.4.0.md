# Giggle 64 — Alpha 1.4.0: The Legend of Bun

A new story scene. When Gigglebuns draws the Bun Sword from its stone, he
learns where the sword came from, and where he came from, before Castle Town
falls.

## New scene: the Legend

- **Lothelye appears.** As the sword leaves the stone, the Hidden Forest goes
  quiet and dark, and a column of light comes down over the pedestal.
  Lothelye, the Cosmic Deity who dreamed the world, appears in it as a
  glowing vision: long bright white hair, a gold circlet with a star, and a
  white and gold gown whose hem fades into a starry night sky.
- **Her dream.** She takes Gigglebuns into a starry void and shows him the
  legend of the world, acted out in light:
  - a world swelling out of a point of light, bare and silent at first;
  - the three goddesses as figures of colored light: **Alan** (green)
    brings forests and fields, **Aphiel** (blue) brings skies and rivers,
    and **Serielye** (red) brings the hells and the mountains of fire;
  - the goddesses rising to the heavens, where they shine as three stars;
  - the Dark Lord **Denangdyn** rising from the hells as a towering shadow,
    darkening the world;
  - the **Nameless hero** called out of the light. The three goddesses'
    stars pour into his blade, making the **Magical Sword of Bun**, and
    he seals Denangdyn in the **Astral Dimension**;
  - the **Sapphire Order** slipping away into the dark.
- **The line of heroes.** The sword wakes once in every age for one of the
  hero's own blood. Gigglebuns the First, the Second and the Third appear one
  by one in gold, and then they all turn to the one at the end of the line:
  **Gigglebuns the Fourth**.
- **A warning.** Lothelye tells him that someone holding a stolen fire felt
  the sword wake too. Then he wakes at the pedestal, and the takeover of
  Castle Town begins.

## Changes

- New character model: Lothelye, with floating, speaking, arms-raised,
  reaching, blessing and sorrowful poses.
- New sounds: Lothelye's appearance (a choir and rising bells), the
  goddesses' chime, and a slow choir loop for her dream.
