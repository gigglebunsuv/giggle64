# Beta 1.2.0 — The Fall of Bunsriel

Chapter IV, complete. The Dark Prophecy comes true: the Citadel of Haven's fortresses fall one by one, Haven Village becomes the last refuge, and the sleeping sword finally wakes.

## New: Chapter IV, "The Fall of Bunsriel"
- **The war begins**: after Ottilie reads the Dark Prophecy, a soldier of the Haven Guard runs into the library. Northwatch is burning.
- **Bunsriel Fields at war**: the field is now under a smoky sky, scorched in great black swathes, with fires everywhere and Firebuns' banners on the castle road. Goblin patrols, archers and **boar riders** roam the field.
- **Haven, the last refuge**: tents fill the plaza, there's a field kitchen, and a timber palisade now runs across the village's north side. Meet **Captain Rowena** of the Haven Guard.
- **Three goblin war camps** to break on the field. When the last one falls, Eastgate's beacon goes out...
- **The Siege of Haven**: a night defense of the village wall in four waves.
  - Goblins throw scaling ladders up the palisade; cut their ropes from inside before they get a foothold.
  - Siege towers roll up the road, war boars ram the gate and archers fire from the slope. Man the **ballista** on the gatehouse.
  - **Aladriel** holds the east of the wall and **Idrith** the west, fighting at your side.
  - Watch the **WEST** and **EAST** hold meters and the **GATE**. If a side breaks, the gate falls or you fall, the wave starts again. Between waves, mend the gate's loose planks with the **Iron Mallet**.
  - In the last wave, an Order battle-mage comes over the wall.
- **Westmarch Fortress**: a new dungeon with 19 rooms on four storeys.
  - Treasures: the **Anchor Boots** and the **Sapphire Staff**.
  - Mini-boss: the Order battle-mage. Boss: **Grukk the Siegebreaker**.
  - Idrith and Aladriel hold out in the great hall, and three lost soldiers wait in the cells.
- **The Dark Forest**, south of the desert: a maze of black woods. Wrong turns lead back to the start, and the true road is marked by **star-stones** that only show in the Starlight Lantern's light. At its heart is the sacred grove, and something buried there.
- **The Temple of Bun**: a new dungeon with 20 rooms.
  - The trial of the pure heart: your own shadow, **Dark Gigglebuns**.
  - Treasure: the **Starfall Arrows**. Boss: **the Pedestal Guardian**.
  - At the temple's heart, the **Bun Sword wakes**, and Lothelye gives you the **Astral Seal**.
- **The Burning Citadel**: the Castle of Haven, now on fire inside. It has 31 rooms on six storeys, the largest dungeon yet, with lava, fire jets and flame wards.
  - Treasure: the **Long Grapple**.
  - On the throne-room roof, **Firebuns III**. This time, you win.
- **The peace**, and END OF CHAPTER IV.

## New items and gear
- **Grave Spade**: lent by Wren in Ashwick. Dig soft earth for buried things, and now and then turn up a Coin or a heart anywhere.
- **Sapphire Staff** (uses magic): conjure a great sapphire block. It holds down weight plates, you can stand on it, it blocks fire jets, and charging foes smash into it. Use the staff again to dispel it.
- **Starfall Arrows** (uses magic): open star eyes, put out the Order's wards, light star braziers, and break flame that nothing else can touch.
- **Astral Seal**: a crystal of the Astral Dimension.
- **Long Grapple**: your Grapple Hook's chain is now twice as long.
- **Anchor Boots**: sink in deep water and walk along the bottom; no wind can move you. Without the Tide Tunic you can only hold your breath for a while.
- **Bun Sword (Awakened)**: it hits much harder (6), and its starlight beam fires at any health and puts out wards.
- New upgrades: Bigger Bomb Bag (30), Bigger Puff Nut Pouch (30), Bigger Quiver, Purple Coins (50) and Gold Coins (100).
- The empty SELECT ITEM sockets for these items are now filled.

## Side quests and smaller things
- **The Lost Garrison**: find Rowena's eight missing soldiers in the war camps, out on the field and in Westmarch's cells. She rewards every second soldier you send home, ending with Pink gilding all your shields in gold.
- **The Buried Charts**: find six scraps of an old treasure chart, then dig where each X marks the spot. The hints are kept in your Journal.
- **Where is Pepper?**: little Posy's dog is hiding in a goblin camp. Find him and he'll follow you home.
- **Rebuilding Castle Town**: after the peace, give Coins to Marigold's Rebuilding Fund. The market, the fountain, the homes and Pink's Armory come back one at a time.
- **The Bestiary**: a new Journal page listing every creature you've beaten, how many, and the toughest rank you met it at.
- A memorial to the fallen in the refuge.
- After the peace, Elder Maud and Tamsin come to live in Haven.
- New title-screen scene: Haven's wall at night during the siege, as the fortress beacons go out.
- Four new Heart Shards, for 28 in all.

## Changed
- **Enemies get tougher in later dungeons.** Each dungeon has a tier; creatures in later ones have more health and hit harder, and tough ones look a little darker. Bosses are unchanged.
- **Pink won't forge the Red Sword until the Southern Tower is cleared**, because the ore can't reach her while the goblins hold the south road. The Magical Sword waits on **Ladhellin Tower**, because the moon-silver comes with the desert caravans.
- After Castle Town falls, Pink forges and sells swords and shields from Himeko's palace.
- Westmarch Fortress now stands on the field's south-west hills (its gate opens once the war begins).

## Fixed
- **Skyreach Spire's wind gaps**: taking the Wind Boots off and leaping into a gale now carries you across. The wind takes you further in the air and slows your fall while you ride it. The void under the tower's pits is also lower, so a leap that falls a little short can still catch the far ledge, where before even a small dip voided you out.
