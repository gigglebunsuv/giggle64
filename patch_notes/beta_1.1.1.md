# Beta 1.1.1 — Chapter and Verse

The story now announces its chapters, and the credits wait for the real end of the game.

## New: chapter title cards
- Each chapter opens on a title card over black:
  - **CHAPTER I — THE GAUNTLET OF FIRE** when you start a new game, before Sir Gigglebuns IV wakes up.
  - **CHAPTER II — THE GAUNTLET'S TRAIL** on the morning after the battle at the castle.
  - **CHAPTER III — THE BATELA FELLS** once you've read the Order's map in the Sun Trial.
- Chapters end on an **END OF CHAPTER** card: after Firebuns' victory at the castle (Chapter I) and after Ottilie reads the Dark Prophecy (Chapter III).
- The file select now shows which chapter each file has reached.

## Changed
- **No more credits after the first fight with Firebuns.** That ending is now the end of Chapter I. The credits will roll at the very end of the game. Until then you can watch them under OPTIONS > CREDITS.

## Fixed
- **The Hidden Forest pedestal** was hard to reach because it sat right at the edge of its island. The island is now wider, so there's dry ground all round the pedestal and the last stepping stone reaches the shore.
