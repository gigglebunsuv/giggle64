# Beta 1.3.0 — Firebuns Returns

Chapter V begins. Years have passed, and Haven is at peace... until the ice in the Astral Dimension breaks. This update is the first half of the chapter: the knighting, the Knight's Techniques, the Barrow of Kings, Cinderhold and the Cinder Vault. Black Water Ridge and the end of Saga IV come in the next updates.

## New: Chapter V, "Firebuns Returns" (first half)
- **The summons**: after the peace, a letter from the castle waits on your table at home. Read it whenever you're ready (finish the Rebuilding Fund and anything else first if you like). Then the years pass...
- **The knighting**: Castle Town is finished at last, banners on every tower. Commander Rowena knights you in the castle yard, and Princess Himeko insists on doing the sword-tap herself. You are now **Knight Gigglebuns**.
- **The Knight's Tabard**: a new vanity outfit in Haven blue and silver, with a blue cape. Wear it over any tunic.
- **Firebuns returns**: the Astral Seal in your pack cracks. A vision of the ice prison, a vast shadow behind the crack in the dark, and the ice shattering. Firebuns III is free, and **Mara, the Scourge of Bunsriel**, rises beside him.
- **The Barrow of Kings**: a new dungeon with 18 rooms on four storeys, under the old royal chapel east of the market square. Haven's dead kings and their sworn knights have risen.
  - **Barrow Knights** guard with their shields up. Break their guard with the Shield Bash.
  - The kings sealed the way down with **floor seals**, which only a Down Thrust can break.
  - **Rune bells** ring together only to a Great Spin.
  - Treasure: the **Questlord's Gauntlets**. Mini-boss: the **Barrow Champion**. Boss: **the Hollow King**.
- **Cinderhold**: Serielye's old volcano, east past the Northeastern Cliffs. You can drag the great stone off the cliffs' east pass once you have the Gauntlets. Lava hollows, black glass spires, ash and embers, a hot spring at the mountain's foot, and a waystone.
- **The Cinder Vault**: a new dungeon with 18 rooms, where the Gauntlet of Fire was first kept. The upper halls are a burning forge; below lies the frost Serielye's wardens laid over the vault.
  - Treasure: the **Ember Rod**. Mini-boss: the **Vault Warden**. Boss: **Magmaw, the Lava Serpent**. Its skin turns the sword until a Frost Arrow crusts it over.
- At the Vault's heart you arrive too late. **Serielye's echo** is watching, and she has something to say to you. The road leads west, to **Black Water Ridge**, but the black water rising in the marshes still blocks the way (until the next update).

## New: the Knight's Techniques
Commander Rowena trains you in the new **Knights' Yard** on the castle's west lawn, with a trial for each technique:
- **Great Spin**: after a swing, keep holding **B**. When the blade shines, let go and it sweeps all the way round you, twice as hard.
- **Down Thrust**: press **B** while dropping from a height (off a ledge, for instance). The blade goes straight down, and the shock where you land hits and dazes everything around you. It also breaks cracked floor seals.
- **Shield Bash**: hold your shield up (**RT**) and press **A** to shove. It knocks foes back and staggers them, and it breaks guards that no blade can get through.
- Pass all three trials for a Heart Shard.

## New items and gear
- **Ember Rod** (uses a little magic): throws a fireball. It lights torches, burns away thorns and webs, and melts rime-ice. The undead burn twice as hard; creatures of fire don't burn at all.
- **Questlord's Gauntlets** (strength level 2): take hold of a **great stone** with A and drag it away.
- **Knight's Tabard** (vanity outfit).
- The Ember Rod fills its empty socket on the SELECT ITEM screen.

## Old-world secrets
With the Gauntlets and the Ember Rod, places you passed long ago open up:
- A sealed fire door on the Northeastern Cliffs with two cold braziers.
- A hollow choked with black thorns in the Forest.
- Great stones in the Desert of Ladhellin's north cliffs and on Bunsriel Fields' north edge.
- A cave frozen shut in rime-ice on Frostpeak.
- Rewards: Heart Shards, a Bigger Bomb Bag and a Bigger Puff Nut Pouch.
- There are now **32** Heart Shards in all (Ottilie's fourth bottle still needs every one).

## Smaller things
- **Pepper, all grown up**: gray round the muzzle and twice the size, he lives with Posy in Castle Town and trots at your heels round Haven. Now and then he digs up a Coin. Talk to him to tell him to stay or to come along.
- **The Kings of Haven**: each of the seven tombs in the Barrow has an epitaph. Read them all to fill a new Journal page, and the kings will open what they kept together.
- **Cinderfin**: a new fish in Cinderhold's hot spring (and anywhere else you fish there). It goes on Mira's board.
- **New title-screen scene**: the knighting at dawn on the castle avenue.
- **Knight Gigglebuns** now stands on the title screen, sword raised.
- Castle Town's people have had a few years too. Posy is nearly grown, and Marigold thinks you look thin.
- The Bestiary has new creatures: Barrow Knights, Cinderlings, Ember Knights, the Vault Warden, the Hollow King and Magmaw.

## Changed
- The undead (Skeletons of every kind, Mummies, Shades, the Barrow's dead) take double damage from the Ember Rod.
- The Journal and the file select now know about Chapter V.
- The debug menu's SCENES tab can replay the knighting, Firebuns' return and the Cinder Vault's ending, and its give-all cheat now includes the Knight's Techniques.
