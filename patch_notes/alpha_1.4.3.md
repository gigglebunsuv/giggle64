# Alpha 1.4.3 - Light Through the Smoke

A small fix for the Silvered Shield puzzles in the Castle.

## Fixed
- **West Rampart mirror puzzle**: the bronze mirror used to start with its back to the sunlight, so the light just stopped dead on it and the puzzle looked broken. It now starts facing the light (throwing it straight back at you), and two turns swing it round onto the sun carved on the tower wall.
- **Finicky aiming**: light thrown off the Silvered Shield, or bounced off a mirror, now locks onto a nearby mirror or sun face when you aim within about 12 degrees of it. You no longer have to line it up to the degree from across the room. (This applies everywhere, Ladhellin Tower included.)
- **Bigger sun pools** in the Chapel and on the West Rampart, so you don't step out of the light while you aim.
