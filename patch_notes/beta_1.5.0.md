# Beta 1.5.0: The Astral Rift

The last chapter of Saga IV, and the end of the game. Giggle 64 is now feature complete; from here on, updates are fixes and polish.

## The end of Chapter V
- **The crack in the sky.** Come back down from Ridge Keep after Mara, and Commander Rowena is waiting under the broken sky with news: the last of the Sapphire Order have dug in at the old chapel in the Batela Fells.
- **The Sanctum Ruin.** The top of the Sapphire Sanctum has fallen in. Fight through the Order's last guard to the ritual circle under the open sky, and face **Archmagus Velloran**. His ward turns every blade; Lothelye's light breaks it. An Ashwick scout is locked in the Order's cages.
- **Lothelye's Dream**, the last spell. Lothelye waits at her spring in the West Marshlands with her final gift. Cast it and you dream awake: paths of dream-light hold you up and the veils of the waking world fall away. It draws magic for as long as you dream.
- **The Astral Rift**, the last and largest dungeon: 40 rooms of floating "memory islands" in the starless dark, pieces of every place you have been. Five drifts of islands hang far apart; rings of starlight carry you between them.
  - The Shore of Stars: the lake's memory, and the great hall with its four star locks.
  - The Spires of Memory: Skyreach's wind over the void, and the Grapple Hook.
  - The Burning Halls: the Citadel's fire, the Sapphire Staff and starlight.
  - The Barrow of Night: great stones, bells for the Iron Flail, the boss key.
  - The Heart: a starfield you can only cross by dreaming, a hall of remembrance, a fountain, and Avalon's garden.
- **Avalon, Keeper of the Stars.** Before the heart of the Rift, Avalon heals you and blesses the Bun Sword with the Luminous Blade's starlight. Her scene is timed to her theme.
- **The true final battle** against Firebuns III, in three phases:
  1. **Flame.** His flame shield is back, hotter. Break it with Starfall Arrows.
  2. **The Gauntlet.** Meteors fall where you stand, and a great orb hunts you. Strike it back with your sword and keep the rally going until he misses.
  3. **The shadow.** Denangdyn's shadow pours into him, and two shadow hands fight for him. Stun both, with a blessed blow to a stuck palm or a Parry of a sweep, and he is laid bare. Only starlight can wound him now.
- **The ending.** Serielye appears. The credits are a tour of Bunsriel at peace, timed to the credits music, followed by a dedication and a glimpse of what comes next.

## The beginning
- **A prologue.** After the Chapter I card, far away in the Cinder Vault, Firebuns III walks through the dark to the Gauntlet of Fire, frozen in Serielye's ice.
- **Pip**, page to the King, now wakes you on your first morning with the King's summons and the first step of the road. Pip becomes Castle Town's herald and cries the latest news by the fountain in every age of the town.

## Game over
- Falling with no fairy in a bottle now brings up **GAME OVER**, with its own theme: **Continue**, **Save & Continue** or **Save & Quit**. Continue puts you back where you came into the area (a dungeon's entrance) with three hearts.

## Castle Town, Haven and Ashwick
- **Most houses can be entered.** Two of every three houses in Castle Town (28), six cottages in Haven Village and three tents in Ashwick. Inside are bakers, tailors, a cartographer, a toymaker, a retired captain, a widow and her eleven cats, newlyweds and twins.
  - Every household has something new to say in each age of the story.
- **The Golden Bun**, Castle Town's tavern, has a house band. After the true ending, they finally play their own song.
- **The market is busy again.** Each stall has a keeper with something for sale (fruit, arrows, honey buns, red potions), and there are people passing through.

## Finding your way, in the world
- **People talk about what's happening.** Every other time you speak to a townsperson, they pass on the talk of the day, which is always your next step. Pip cries it as news.
- **The guiding light.** Out in the open, a column of pale gold light stands on the road to your next goal: at the right exit, or at the door of the place itself. It can be switched off in OPTIONS (GUIDING LIGHT).
- **Opening shots.** When the story opens a way somewhere else, the camera shows it the first time you come back.
- **First views.** The first time you enter a great region, the camera rises and looks out to where you're meant to go.
- **The goddesses speak** when you take each piece of the Bun, and point you to the next. At Ladhellin Tower, Serielye herself appears for a moment.
- After the Lake Shrine, a short scene points you to the first goddess tower.

## Music (MIDI)
- New: astral_rift (A major on Avalon's motif, with an F# minor section), velloran_battle, true_final_battle, final_hope (phase 3: Avalon's motif and the main motif together), ending_cutscene (Serielye), prologue, game_over, tavern.

## Other
- Serielye now appears in her own form at her Goddess Spring and as her echo in the Cinder Vault. In Lothelye's legend she stays an orange figure of light.
- A finished file shows a gold star and THE END on the file select.
- Full credits, with special thanks and a dedication.
- The debug menu's SCENES tab can play every new scene.
- The Sand Treads are cut.

## After the ending
- Load your finished file and you wake at home to a peaceful morning. Every road is still open, the people you know have something to say about what you did, and the band at the Golden Bun has a new song.

## Fixed
- **The journal now lists the Bog Tower** in Chapter I. The castle needs what's inside (the Bloom Rod), but the journal used to skip straight from the goddess towers to the Bun Sword. The townsfolk, Pip and the guiding light all point there now too, and so does Aphiel.
- You could squeeze past the side of the frozen falls on Frostpeak and get into Frostpeak Hollow without breaking the ice. The way in now stays shut until the Iron Mallet breaks it.
- **Knight side swings no longer hit you behind their back.** The Tide Knight and its kin used to whip round as they wound up the sweep, so standing behind one got you hit anyway. Now they turn only part of the way, the swing's reach stops at their hips, and, as with OoT's Iron Knuckle, they only swing at your back if you stay there for a couple of seconds. It still catches you at their side.
- A message too long for one box now carries on in the next box instead of running off the bottom.
