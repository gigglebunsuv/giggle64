# Beta 1.4.2 — Cloth in the Wind

The knight's cape is real cloth now.

## Changed
- **Capes move like Ganondorf's cape.** The capes on the Knight's Regalia and the Knight's Tabard used to be a stiff panel that tipped back as you ran. Now they're cloth, simulated the same way as Ganondorf's cape in Ocarina of Time: twelve strands hang from a curved collar, each pushed off your back, flared out at the shoulders and pulled down by gravity.
  - It streams out behind you on a run, ripples at a walk, and swings forward past your legs when you stop before it falls still.
  - On a sharp turn it trails behind and whips round after you.
  - It's kept off your legs and off the ground, and drops straight back into place when you warp or change areas instead of flying in after you.
  - It hangs in gentle folds at the collar, and its gold or silver hem now follows the cloth.
- The cape is drawn the N64 way too when TRUE N64 MODE is on.

## Under the hood
- New test: the cape hangs behind you, streams back at running speed and settles again after you stop.
