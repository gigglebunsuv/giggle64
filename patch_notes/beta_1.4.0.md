# Beta 1.4.0 — Black Water Ridge

The second half of Chapter V. The knights of Haven march west, the black water parts, and the war against Mara's undead army is fought on Black Water Ridge. At the top of the falls, Mara herself is waiting. The Astral Rift and the end of Saga IV come in the next update.

## New: Chapter V, "Black Water Ridge"
- **The knights' camp**: after the Cinder Vault, Commander Rowena and her knights pitch their tents on the last dry ground at the **west end of the West Marshlands**, where the black water stops them. Meet Sir Brannoc, Dame Elsabet and the Quartermaster, who restocks your arrows, bombs, Puff Nuts, magic and hearts once per visit.
- **The black water parts**: once you've learned the Parry, Rowena asks for your sword. The awakened Bun Sword's starlight drives the water back, and an old road of white stones leads west.
- **Black Water Ridge**: a new region, a canyon of black rock climbing in switchbacks from the marsh to a keep at the top of the falls. Black falls pour from every ledge into pools that never ripple. It has a waystone by the way in.
- **Ridge Keep**: a new dungeon with 23 rooms on four storeys, climbing up through Mara's fortress to the open roof at the top of the falls.
  - The lower halls are half drowned in black water, and the cells are full of the drowned.
  - Treasure: the **Iron Flail**. Mini-boss: **the Jailer**, a drowned jailer swinging the flail on its chain. Boss: **Mara, the Scourge of Bunsriel**, fought one-on-one at the top of the falls.
  - After the battle: the dead lie down all over the Ridge, the falls run clear... and something cracks open in the sky. A Heart Vessel waits too, which brings you to the full 22 hearts.

## New: the Ridge War Camps
Mara has three camps on the slopes below her keep, and three great chains run from the Keep's gate down to them. While their bone fires burn, the gate stays shut.
- **Two knights of Haven** wait at each camp and fight beside you while you're there.
- Beat every one of the camp's dead and the camp is broken: its green fire goes out, Haven's banner goes up, and a sentry stays to keep watch.
- A freed camp is a **safe point**: rest by its clean fire to refill your hearts. Rowena holds the first one, and she'll tell you how many camps are left.
- Break all three and the chains on the Keep's gate snap.

## New: the Questlord's Greatsword
- **Aladriel's giant sword**, a two-handed blade after Ocarina of Time's Biggoron's Sword. Once you've been knighted, talk to Aladriel in Pink's Armory: Pink has forged her a bigger one, and Idrith says they can't keep two giant swords in the house.
- It's the fifth sword on the EQUIPMENT screen, and it goes on **B** like any other sword.
- **Two-handed**: both hands are on the grip, and your **shield goes on your back** whenever the sword is out. Put it away and the shield comes back to your arm.
- It hits for **8** (the awakened Bun Sword hits for 6) and reaches further, but every swing is a little slower.
- **The shield button guards with the flat of the blade.** It stops blows from in front, heavy ones too. It can't turn projectiles back or block fire, and its guard is narrower than a shield's.
- **No Shield Bash or Parry** with it out. To make up for that, its blows **smash an undead knight's shield aside**, the way a bash does.
- The Great Spin and the Down Thrust work with it.

## New: the Parry
Rowena's fourth Knight's Technique, taught at the knights' camp on a training **quintain** (a post with a swinging arm and a sandbag).
- Shield up (**RT**), and press **A** to bash **just as the blow lands**. A blow you parry bounces off without pushing you back, and the attacker reels with their guard wide open.
- An undead knight's guard breaks for longer than from a plain bash. Mara's fight is built around it.
- Parry the quintain three times in a row to pass the trial.

## New item: the Iron Flail
- In the ARMS row of SELECT ITEM (its empty socket is filled at last). Put it on X or Y and press to hurl the spiked ball straight ahead; it swings back on its chain.
- It hits hard, and it knocks an undead knight's shield aside.
- **Only the flail** bursts old iron grates, topples cracked pillars and rings iron bells hung too high for a sword.

## Flail secrets in old places
The Iron Flail opens places you passed long ago:
- An iron grate in the north cliffs of Bunsriel Fields.
- A Ladhellin strongroom behind a grate in the south cliffs of the Desert of Ladhellin.
- An old fishing store behind a grate by Mirror Lake.
- The **old watch bells** on the Northeastern Cliffs' lowest tier. Ring all three.
- Rewards: Coin hoards. The Heart Shard count doesn't change.

## Smaller things
- **New title-screen scene**: Black Water Ridge at night. The knight, Rowena and Sir Brannoc look up at Ridge Keep.
- **The Ridge's dead are stronger**: one tier above the rest of Haven, and **one more at night**. Dame Elsabet advises fighting by day.
- **A memorial banner** in Castle Town's square after Mara falls, with the names of the knights lost on the Ridge.
- The Bestiary knows the Jailer and Mara.
- The Journal's Chapter V now runs to the top of the falls.

## Changed
- Commander Rowena leaves the Knights' Yard once Firebuns leaves the Cinder Vault. She's with her knights in the marshes, and later on the Ridge.
- Pink has something to say if you ask her about a sword bigger than the Bun Sword.
- The debug menu's SCENES tab can replay the black water parting, the arrival on the Ridge, the chains breaking and Mara's fall, plus the new title scene.
