# Beta 1.7.0 — Seal of Quality

A polish and release-readiness update: an instruction booklet, a fresh world map, smoother enemies, new secrets in the old dungeons, a levelled soundtrack, a bug-report key for playtesters and proper release builds.

## The instruction booklet
- **A 36-page booklet at real N64 size** (150 × 108 mm), in the box art's oxblood and gold, with screenshots from the game. It covers:
  - the legend of Bunsriel and the start of the story;
  - the cast;
  - the controller and the keyboard;
  - starting a game, saving and Game Over;
  - the game screen;
  - moving, Lock-On and combat, items, riding and fishing;
  - the pause screen and options;
  - items and gear, shops, dungeons, the map, things to do, Pip's hints and the credits.
- **Two editions** in `promo/manual`:
  - one for reading on screen;
  - one imposed for printing at home: print it double-sided (flip on the short edge), fold the stack and staple it.

## The world map
- **Redrawn to match the game.** The printed map now lays the regions out exactly as the pause screen's MAP page does. The old poster used an earlier arrangement.
- The poster lists every dungeon, Goddess Spring and house door, including the Barrow of Kings, the Sanctum Ruin and the Astral Rift.

## Smoother enemies
- **No more snapping between moves.** Enemies always ease from one animation into the next over at least a few frames, and townsfolk a little more gently. The hero's own timing is unchanged.
- **Mara's thrust** was a single frozen pose. Now she coils, then lunges, with her glaive levelled at you; it used to stand straight up.
- Corvax and the wyverns flutter as they swoop, Firebuns III breathes behind his guard, and Gullagog kicks in mid-leap.

## New secrets in the old dungeons
- **Mallet pegs.** The Southern Tower's den, Ladhellin Tower's shrine and Bog Tower's snare room each have three old pegs in the floor. Come back with the Iron Mallet and drive them down for a chest.
- **Looking Glass chests.** The Lake Shrine's coral hall and the Castle's wine cellar each hide a chest only Mira's Looking Glass shows.

## Sound
- **Every song at one volume.** The quietest and loudest tracks were 18 dB apart: the inside of a house was barely audible, while the boss theme was very loud. Each track now plays at a measured level, close to the rest, without clipping. This covers area music, cutscene scores, the title theme and the title scenes.
- **Three sounds that were much louder than the rest** (the fire whoosh, a stone being gripped, a switch being pressed) are toned down.

## For playtesters
- **F8 saves a bug report.** It goes in a folder under the game's `bug_reports`, with:
  - a screenshot;
  - where you are and what the hero is doing;
  - the time of day, your settings and every story flag;
  - the whole game state as a save file;
  - the log of the session.

  Send the folder with a sentence about what happened.
- **Release builds.** The game now has its own icon (the gold 64 plate) and proper version details on Windows. The debug menu (F1) and the DEBUG file are only in development and beta builds.

## The soundtrack
- **The OST album is complete:** 59 tracks, about 98 minutes. New on it:
  - Prologue, The Golden Bun, Game Over, To Be Continued, Frostpeak Hollow;
  - Archmagus Velloran, The Astral Rift, The Last Flame, Final Hope and Serielye.

## Fixes
- The Purple and Gold Coin messages still said "Rupees".

## Under the hood
- The project is now a git repository, with a README and the promo art.
- **Versioning.** `GameManager.VERSION` / `TAGLINE` drive the title screen line, and `project.godot` carries `1.7.0-beta`.
- **Audio.**
  - `AudioLevels` (`scripts/systems/audio_levels.gd`) is generated by `_giggle64_work/tools/measure_levels.py` from EBU R128 measurements. Re-run it when a track is added.
  - `Sfx.TRIM` trims hot sounds.
- **Animation.** `CharacterModel.min_blend` sets the shortest crossfade. Enemies use `Enemy.ENEMY_MIN_BLEND` (0.08 s) and townsfolk 0.12 s.
- **Debug and bug reports.**
  - `DebugMenu.allowed()` is true in debug builds or with the `g64_debug` export feature, which the beta presets include.
  - `BugReport` (`scripts/systems/bug_report.gd`) handles F8.
- **Promo tools.**
  - `tests/manual_capture.tscn` takes the booklet's screenshots.
  - `--pad-glyphs` shows Xbox icons without a pad attached.
  - `render_character_card.tscn -- --cutout` renders figures on transparent backgrounds.
  - `tools/worldmap/ingame_layout.py` draws the map in the pause screen's layout.
- **New tests:**
  - test_bug_report
  - test_older_dungeon_secrets
  - test_audio_mix_levels
- **Updated test:** test_beta_title now checks `GameManager.VERSION`.
- **Plans for later** (not built) are in `design/`: `randomizer_plan.md`, `boss_rush_plan.md` and `saga_v_plan.md`.
