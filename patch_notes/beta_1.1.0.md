# Beta 1.1.0 — The Batela Fells

Chapter III. The Gauntlet's trail leads east, out of Haven, to the burnt homeland of Firebuns III.

## New: Chapter III, "The Batela Fells"
- **The old east road**: once you have read the Order's map in the Sun Trial, the Eternal Sandstorm on the desert's east edge drops, and the old Batela road opens.
- **The Forest of the Dead**: a lost-woods maze of dead trees and graves. Take a wrong lane and the fog turns you round, back where you started. The wisps of the Batela dead wait at the mouth of the right lane, and they chime when you're close.
- **The Batela Fells**: a new region of ash moors under a gray sky, with ash still falling. There are two dead villages, a black tarn, the burnt bell tower, the old chapel, and the ruins of **Batela Keep** on the north ridge.
- **Ashwick**: the camp of the last Batela survivors, with its own music. You'll meet **Elder Maud**, Wren the gravekeeper, Odile (once a guard of the Keep) and ten-year-old Tamsin.
- **The duel at Batela Keep**: Firebuns III is waiting in his old hall. You can survive this fight, but you can't win it.
- **Maud's confession**: what Batela did to its king, and why the Fells burned.
- **The Sapphire Sanctum**: the Order's hideout under the chapel crypt, a new dungeon. It has 20 rooms on five storeys going down, with each way down a drop through the floor and a ladder back up.
  - Treasure: **the Starlight Lantern**. Lothelye's starlight now burns in your Lantern, a cold blue flame the Gauntlet of Fire cannot command. It puts out the Order's **sapphire wards**, lights **star braziers**, and makes the **shades** hiding in the dark visible.
  - Mini-boss: the Sapphire Warden. Boss: **Incunax, the Bound Page**.
  - Past the boss, the Order's ritual chamber, Archmagus Velloran, and **Bunsriel's Dark Prophecy**.
- Take the Prophecy to **Ottilie** in Haven Library to end the chapter.
- The JOURNAL has the Chapter III quest, and a new side quest, **The Lost Pages**: five pages of the Fari Incunabulum to find.

## New items and gear
- **SELECT ITEM now has 28 slots** (7 x 4). The empty sockets are kept for items still to come.
- **The trade chain**: Clover's Lucky Horseshoe → Pink → Ottilie → Marigold → Mira → Pink, who forges the **Questlord's Shield**. It has the widest guard of any shield, the least push-back, and the Order's fire breaks harmlessly on it. Press the trade item's button to see where it goes next.
- **The fourth bottle**: find every Heart Shard (there are now 24) and show Ottilie.
- Two new Heart Shards: the top of the burnt bell tower, and the Sanctum's library.

## New systems
- **Weather**: now and then a day in Haven is rainy, with rain, a greyer light, the sound of rain, and squelching footsteps on soft ground. The Batela Fells always have falling ash.
- **Waystones**: one in each region. Touch a stone to wake it, then travel between any two awakened stones.
- New footstep sound for ash.

## Music (MIDI, for you to render)

- `credits`: the bittersweet end credits for the end of Saga IV, ready for later.
- `firebuns_theme` now plays in the duel at Batela Keep.

## Smaller things
- Firebuns' portraits hang in Batela Keep's hall (you can Examine them).
- A new fish: the **Tarn Ashfin**, from the black tarn. Mira's fish board now has room for seven.
- A new title-screen scene: Ashwick's fire at dusk, and a light on the old keep.
- Mira, Himeko and Ottilie have new things to say about the Fells and the Prophecy.
- The world map is wider, to fit the lands east of the desert.
- The credits list Ashwick's people and the Order.
