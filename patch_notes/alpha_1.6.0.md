# Giggle 64 — Alpha 1.6.0: The Satchel

The item list is final: 20 slots, made up of 16 items and 4 bottles. Ten new
items are in the game, the SELECT ITEM screen has been redesigned, and the
hero now has a magic meter.

## A new SELECT ITEM screen

- **20 slots in four sections**, one row each:
  - **ARMS:** Bow, Sun Arrows, Frost Arrows, Bombs, Puff Nuts. Everything that uses ammo.
  - **TOOLS:** Boomerang, Grapple Hook, Bloom Rod, Iron Mallet, Fishing Rod.
  - **MAGIC:** Lantern, Mira's Looking Glass, Alan's Harvest, Aphiel's Gale, Serielye's Ruin.
  - **PACK:** the four bottles and the Sugar Cube.
- **Redesigned in the style of Ocarina of Time:** a carved slate page in a gold
  frame with corner studs, and a red title banner. Each section sits in its own
  sunken tray with a name plate, and items rest in round sockets.
- OoT's four-cornered cursor breathes in and out, and the item under it is
  drawn larger.
- Items on X and Y get OoT's gold outline and their button badge. A gold ring
  bursts out when you equip one.
- Items that use magic carry a small green gem. The name panel shows their
  magic cost.
- Every pause page (Map, Quest Status, Options, Equipment) uses the new frame
  and banner.

## Magic

- **A magic meter.** The first magic item you find adds OoT's green meter
  under your hearts. When you spend magic, a yellow strip shows how much
  was used.
- **Magic jars** drop from grass, pots and enemies once you have the meter.
  A small jar restores 12, a large one fills the meter.

## New items

- **Sun Arrows** (8 magic each): fired from the bow, they hit hard and burst
  into light. They wake sun switches from afar and reveal Shades.
- **Frost Arrows** (4 magic each): they freeze enemies solid, and the next hit
  shatters the ice for double damage. Shot into open water, they freeze a floe
  you can stand on for 25 seconds.
- **Puff Nuts:** a quick throw that bursts in a flash of spores and dazes every
  enemy nearby. The pouch holds 20. Sold by Marigold; they also drop from grass
  and enemies.
- **Iron Mallet:** an overhead smash two steps ahead. It breaks brittle crust,
  boulders and pots, and hits for 3. The ground shakes and briefly stuns nearby
  enemies.
- **Fishing Rod:** fish from any shore with open water ahead, not just Mira's
  dock. Mira makes you one after you land the big fish.
- **Lantern:** hangs from your belt and lights the dark around you. Use it next
  to an unlit torch to light the torch. Ottilie gives you her spare when you
  visit the library a second time.
- **Mira's Looking Glass:** look through it in first person to see hidden
  things. It drains magic while you use it. Mira gives it to you at night,
  once you've opened the moonlit chest. One hidden thing is waiting at Mirror
  Lake already.
- **Alan's Harvest** (12 magic): a ring of green light that heals three hearts
  and makes nearby bushes give up their loot.
- **Aphiel's Gale** (12 magic): the first use sets a Gale Point. Use it again in
  the same area to warp back to the point, or to dispel it.
- **Serielye's Ruin** (12 magic): a blast of force around you that knocks
  enemies back and breaks brittle things.
- Sun and Frost Arrows also work on horseback.

## Cut from the item plan

Burrow Mitts, Hopper Bombs, Honey Buns (as a carried item) and the trading
sequence item. Idrith's honey buns are still sold in the shop.

## Other changes

- Marigold's counter now has nine wares (Puff Nuts were added).
- New sounds: the spell cast, the mallet's blow, a Puff Nut's pop, the Looking
  Glass, ice forming, the gale's wind, a Sun Arrow's burst and the magic jar
  refill.
- Items in the debug menu: Puff Nut and magic counters, and GIVE EVERYTHING now
  includes the magic meter.
