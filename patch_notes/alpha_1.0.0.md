# Giggle 64 — Alpha 1.0.0: The Goddess Towers

The first alpha. The three great towers of Haven are open, each guarding a
piece of the goddesses' Bun, and together they unlock the Bun Sword.

## New dungeons

### Southern Tower (rebuilt)
- Now four floors deep (cellar to roof) with 13 rooms, up from one floor and 6 rooms.
- The flooded cistern and rat den in the cellar, reached by a ladder through the store-room floor.
- The mill's great water wheel is jammed: hit the crystal on its brake to start it.
  The running mill turns the cogs and millstone, lifts the bars on the upper
  floor, and powers moving platforms across the Gear Works pit.
- The Rust Knight now guards the Grapple Hook in the Mill.
- A grapple hook crossing over the Grain Loft's pit leads to the Boss Key.
- New enemies: **Rats** and **Scarecrow Archers** (their straw bolts can be blocked or reflected with the shield).
- Corvax now guards **Alan's Bun Piece** on the roof.

### Ladhellin Tower (new)
- The desert tower is open: 5 floors and 19 rooms, from the crypt up to the Sun Chamber.
- **Silvered Shield** found inside, won from the **Mummy Lord**.
- **Light puzzles:** stand in a shaft of sunlight with the Silvered Shield raised to
  throw the beam. Turn bronze mirrors to bend it, and light up sun switches to open the way.
- **Quicksand:** wade through it slowly; stand still and it swallows you.
- New enemies: **Mummies** (fire burns them), **Shades** (only sunlight reveals them),
  **Sand Beetles**, and the **Sentinel** mini-boss.
- Boss: **Kherash, the Sand Devourer**, a wyrm that swims through sand. Blind it with
  reflected sunlight, then strike. It guards **Serielye's Bun Piece**.

### Skyreach Spire (new)
- The Frost Shrine on Frostpeak is now the gatehouse of Skyreach Spire: 7 floors and
  24 rooms, from the ice caves to its open crown above the clouds.
- **Wind Boots** found inside, won from the **Frost Knight**.
- **Gales** blow you toward drops, **ice floors** make you slide, and wind vanes can
  be struck to turn the wind.
- New enemies: **Frost Idols**, **Ice Bats**, **Wind Wisps**, and **Frostwing** the ice wyvern mini-boss.
- Boss: **Aeolos, the Storm Eye**. Stand firm in its gale and shoot its open eye.
  It guards **Aphiel's Bun Piece**.

## New items and gear
- **Bun Pieces:** three goddess pieces, shown on the Quest Status screen. Bring all
  three to the pedestal in the Hidden Forest to draw the **Bun Sword**.
  (The old rune-stone route no longer wakes the sword.)
- **Silvered Shield:** now found in Ladhellin Tower; Pink no longer sells it.
- **Wind Boots:** the Equipment screen has a new **Boots** row.
  - On the ground they hold you firm against any wind.
  - **Hold A in the air to glide**, and gliding rides the wind.
- 3 new Heart Shards (Ladhellin, Skyreach, and one on Frostpeak only reachable by gliding).

## Combat
- **Shield aiming:** while crouched behind your shield, the stick tilts and turns it.
  It now guards the direction you aim it.
- **Shield stab:** press B while crouched behind the shield to thrust your sword.
- **Dodging fixed:** Skeletons and knights now commit to their swing instead of
  tracking you through it. A backflip or sidehop on the windup dodges them, as in Ocarina of Time.

## Maps and menus
- Dungeon maps now show one floor at a time; press up/down on the map screen to change floor.
- The minimap follows the floor you're on.
- Quest Status shows your Bun Pieces and the new dungeons.

## Other
- Small Key, Map and Compass texts now fit every dungeon.
- Saves from before this update keep their progress. A Southern Tower already beaten
  counts as beaten, and you receive Alan's Bun Piece.

## Fixes
- **locking on** no longer locks onto enemies through walls, floors or ceilings
  (for example, rats in the Southern Tower room below you).
- **Rats** only notice you when you're on their floor and in sight, so they no
  longer chase you from the room beneath your feet.
