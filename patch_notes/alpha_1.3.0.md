# Giggle 64 — Alpha 1.3.0: Under the Stars

Two new scenes for the title screen. When the title music ends, the game now
picks one of three scenes at random, and it never plays the same one twice
in a row.

## New title scenes

- **Mirror Lake, at dusk.** Mira sits alone at the end of her fishing dock
  as the sun goes down, talks to the lake about nobody ever coming by, catches
  a very small fish and lets it go, and sees the first star come out.
- **The Desert of Ladhellin, at night.** Idrith and Aladriel walk up a dune
  hand in hand to a campfire, sit together on a fallen column under the
  stars, remember Dailah's sandstorm, and share a kiss on the cheek and a
  shooting star. Ladhellin Tower stands behind them, and the oasis lies below.
- The original scene (sword practice and the rat) is still one of the three.

## How the scenes play

- The lines in these scenes **play by themselves**. Each text box stays up
  long enough to read and then moves on, so you don't need to press anything.
- Press any button to skip a scene and go back to the title, the same as
  before.
- Both scenes are set at times of day you can't see in the game yet: a
  sunset sky at the lake, and a dark sky full of stars with a moon over the
  desert. Neither area changes when you play.

## Changes

- New poses for Mira (sitting on the dock with her rod, looking up, holding a
  fish) and for Idrith and Aladriel (walking hand in hand, stargazing,
  leaning in, a kiss).
