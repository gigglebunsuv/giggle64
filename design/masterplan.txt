GIGGLE 64: THE GAUNTLET OF FIRE  -  MASTERPLAN
To the end of Saga IV
Written 2026-10-13, at the start of Beta 1.1.0.

This is the plan for everything still to come, up to the end of Saga IV.
Use it as a reference when adding something; it is not a promise of exact
rooms or numbers. Each update builds only its own chapter. Change anything
here freely, and when a chapter is built, mark it BUILT.

Lore source: The Complete Story of the Kingdom of Bunsriel (Saga IV, Arcs I-III).
The chapters below follow it closely from here on.

==========================================================================
1. THE STORY TO THE END OF SAGA IV
==========================================================================

The thread under all of it:
  Firebuns III's "deal with a dark and powerful entity" (lore, Arc I) was a
  deal with what is left of DENANGDYN. Serielye destroyed his body long ago
  (Saga I), but a remnant of him still sits in the Astral Dimension. The
  Sapphire Order made that deal for Firebuns: they keep his soul alive, and
  he burns the way open for their master. Each chapter peels back a layer.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------
CHAPTER III  "THE BATELA FELLS"                      (Beta 1.1.0, BUILT)
Saga IV Arc I: the land Firebuns ruled, poisoned and burned.
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  1. The Order's map leads east past the Sun Trial into the FOREST OF THE
     DEAD: a Lost Woods-style maze of dead trees and grave mounds between
     Ladhellin and the Fells. Wrong turns loop you back to the entrance.
     Wisps of the Batela dead drift along the right paths; watch them and
     listen for the bells.
  2. Out of the forest: the BATELA FELLS. Decrepit, burnt upland moors under
     ash-fall: dead villages, charred pines, a black tarn, the ruins of
     Batela Keep on the ridge.
  3. ASHWICK, the survivors' camp: the last of the Batela people, who fled
     when Firebuns burned his own land. It is the hub (shop, inn bed,
     waystone, quests). Elder Maud runs it.
  4. Firebuns' heraldry hangs in the Keep ruins: Firebuns I, the Evil King;
     Firebuns II, who "died a hero"; and Firebuns III's empty frame.
  5. Elder Maud admits it: her people poisoned Firebuns III in his sleep.
     She thinks the burning was revenge for that, and that she brought it
     on everyone.
  6. Batela Keep: Firebuns III is there, waiting. A duel you survive but
     cannot win (the sword is still asleep). He leaves you alive on purpose:
     "Go home, little hero. Watch it burn."
  7. Under the Fells: the SAPPHIRE SANCTUM (dungeon), the Order's hideout,
     where they study the Fari Incunabulum. The Order has taken command of
     its torches and braziers (the Gauntlet answers them). You find
     Lothelye's starlight in the Sanctum's lowest vault, and your Lantern
     becomes the STARLIGHT LANTERN: a cold blue flame the Gauntlet cannot
     command.
  8. Mini-boss: the Sapphire Warden. Boss: INCUNAX, THE BOUND PAGE, a beast
     torn out of the Incunabulum.
  9. Ending: Archmagus Velloran, the Order's leader, finishes part of the
     ritual. The Astral seal CRACKS, and a power surges into Firebuns III
     across the whole world. The hero takes a copy of BUNSRIEL'S DARK
     PROPHECY from the Sanctum. Ottilie reads it at the start of Chapter IV.
  Side: Incunabulum pages (5) to collect, the trade chain to the Questlord's
  Shield, the fourth bottle, waystones, weather.

CHAPTER IV  "THE FALL OF BUNSRIEL"         (Beta 1.2.0, BUILT, Saga IV Arc II)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  As built (2026-10-13). The whole chapter went into one update.
  1. Ottilie reads the Dark Prophecy (END OF CHAPTER III), then the CHAPTER IV
     card, and a soldier of the Haven Guard runs into the library: Northwatch
     is burning ("ch4:war"). From now until the sealing, the field is
     field_war (smoke, burns, fires, goblin patrols, boar riders, archers)
     and the village is village_refuge (tents, a palisade across its north
     side, the gatehouse ballista).
  2. Captain Rowena of the Haven Guard, in the refuge: break the three goblin
     WAR CAMPS on the field. The last camp broken, Eastgate's beacon goes out.
  3. THE SIEGE OF HAVEN (the update's major addition): a night defense of the
     palisade in four waves (ladders; siege towers, war boars and archers,
     with the ballista; all at once; an Order battle-mage, who flees west).
     Aladriel holds the east of the wall, Idrith the west (Ally). West/east
     HOLD meters and the gate's health; a lost wave starts again. Between
     waves the gate's planks are mended with the Iron Mallet.
  4. WESTMARCH FORTRESS (19 rooms): the Anchor Boots (armory), the Sapphire
     Staff (the battle-mage, mini-boss), GRUKK THE SIEGEBREAKER (boss, dazed by
     charging into walls or Staff blocks). Three soldiers in its cells. After
     Grukk, Firebuns' fire falls on it: the Questlords bring the hero home.
  5. Despair in the refuge; the hero remembers the prophecy's last line (the
     grove). The brambles on the desert's south-east track open. Wren in
     Ashwick lends the GRAVE SPADE (and the first treasure chart).
  6. THE DARK FOREST: a 4 x 4 maze of black woods; STAR-STONES mark the true
     road, but only in the Starlight Lantern's light. The sacred grove: dig
     out the buried stair.
  7. THE TEMPLE OF BUN (20 rooms): the trial of the pure heart (DARK
     GIGGLEBUNS kneels; strike it and it rises whole, spare it and it fades)
     -> the STARFALL ARROWS; the PEDESTAL GUARDIAN (boss: Starfall Arrow in
     its eye). At the heart: Firebuns II's ghost (ice and starlight), the
     sword laid on the old pedestal wakes (BUN SWORD (AWAKENED): damage 6,
     a starlight beam at any health that puts out wards), and Lothelye gives
     the ASTRAL SEAL.
  8. THE BURNING CITADEL (31 rooms, six storeys, the largest yet): the
     LONGSHOT; fire jets (Staff blocks stop them), lava, flame wards, the
     Order's last guard (Boss Key).
  9. Firebuns III on the throne-room roof: a FLAME SHIELD only Starfall
     Arrows break; the awakened sword wounds him; the Astral Seal freezes him
     and carries him into the Astral Dimension ("ch4:sealed").
 10. The peace: "Though peace would not last forever...", END OF CHAPTER IV,
     wake at home. Castle Town is rebuilt district by district (the
     Rebuilding Fund); Ashwick's people come to live in Haven.
  Side (built): the lost garrison (8 soldiers; Bomb Bag 30, a bigger quiver,
  a Piece of Heart, gilded shields), the buried treasure charts (6 scraps,
  6 dig spots), Pepper the dog, the Bestiary, the memorial in the refuge, the
  Haven siege title scene, dungeon enemy tiers (EnemyScale).
  Not built: the Sand Treads (cut in Beta 1.5.0).

CHAPTER V  "FIREBUNS RETURNS"                       (Saga IV Arc III)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
  FIRST HALF: Beta 1.3.0 "Firebuns Returns" (BUILT 2026-10-13, see section 8).
  SECOND HALF: Beta 1.4.0 (Ridge, Mara) and Beta 1.5.0 (the end) BUILT.
  As built:
  a. After the peace, a summons from the castle waits on the hero's table at
     home. Read it when ready (nothing is missed: the peace's side quests stay
     open until then): "THE YEARS PASSED IN PEACE..." over black, the CHAPTER V
     card, and the KNIGHTING in the castle yard of a Castle Town finished long
     ago (castle_town_restored; every district rebuilt). Commander Rowena
     knights him in the Crown's name; Himeko insists on doing the
     sword-tap herself. KNIGHT'S TABARD (vanity: Haven blue and silver, a cape).
     Flags "ch5:knight".
  b. KNIGHT'S TECHNIQUES (the update's major addition): Rowena trains the new
     knight in the KNIGHTS' YARD beside the castle gate, a trial for each:
       GREAT SPIN   hold B to gather strength, let go: a full turn of the
                    blade that strikes all round, twice as hard ("tech:spin")
       DOWN THRUST  B while dropping from a height: the blade driven down,
                    a shock where it lands; breaks cracked floor seals
                    ("tech:thrust")
       SHIELD BASH  A with the shield up: a shove behind the shield that
                    knocks foes back and breaks an undead knight's guard
                    ("tech:bash")
     All three learned: a Piece of Heart, and then the sky cracks.
  c. FIREBUNS RETURNS (cutscene): the Astral Seal in the pack splits; a vision
     of the ice prison in starless dark, the remnant of Denangdyn's shadow
     behind the crack, the ice shattering. Firebuns III laughs. MARA rises
     beside him. The ground under Haven shakes: the royal crypt has opened
     ("ch5:returned", "ch5:barrow_open"). The Astral Seal is "cracked" from now.
  d. THE BARROW OF KINGS (dungeon, ~18 rooms, under the castle; the Kings'
     Stair in the castle square): risen Haven kings and their knights. BARROW
     KNIGHTS (shield up: bash it). Floor seals (Down Thrust). Ring switches
     (Great Spin). Prize: the QUESTLORD'S GAUNTLETS (bracers tier 2): grip and
     drag GREAT STONES. Seven kings' tombs with EPITAPHS (journal page). Boss:
     THE HOLLOW KING. Freed, he says where the burning one has gone:
     Cinderhold ("ch5:barrow_done").
  e. CINDERHOLD (region): Serielye's old volcano past the Northeastern
     Cliffs' east pass (a great stone across it: the Gauntlets). Lava rivers,
     obsidian, steam, ash-fall, a hot spring at its foot (CINDERFIN), a waystone.
  f. THE CINDER VAULT (dungeon, ~24 rooms): where the Gauntlet of Fire was
     first kept, frozen shut by Serielye's wardens. The EMBER ROD (lights
     torches, burns thorns and webs, melts rime-ice, hurts the undead).
     Boss: MAGMAW, the lava serpent (Frost Arrows crust it, then strike).
  g. At its heart: too late. Firebuns III has rekindled the Gauntlet at the old
     anvil. Mara comes for him. SERIELYE'S ECHO watches, neither friend nor foe,
     and speaks to the hero. They leave west for BLACK WATER RIDGE
     ("ch5:cinder_done"); the black water floods the marshes' west pass until
     Beta 1.4.0 opens it.
  Side (1.3.0): old-world secrets the Gauntlets and the Ember Rod open in
  earlier regions (four Pieces of Heart, Bomb Bag 40, Puff Nut Pouch 40),
  Pepper all grown up, Cinderfin, the knighting title scene + title art.

  The plan for all of Chapter V (from 2026-10-13):
  1. Some years later. The hero is now KNIGHT GIGGLEBUNS: knight's tunic
     and new title-screen art. Haven is rebuilt and at peace. Mira is
     older, and so are the rest of the cast.
  2. Firebuns III breaks out of his icy prison. The remnant of Denangdyn,
     sitting in the same dimension behind the crack the Order made, frees
     him. He returns with an ARMY OF THE UNDEAD led by MARA, THE SCOURGE OF
     BUNSRIEL.
  3. The undead rise first under Haven itself: THE BARROW OF KINGS
     (dungeon), the royal crypt under the castle, the old Haven kings and
     knights risen against their own city. Its prize: the QUESTLORD'S
     GAUNTLETS (bracers tier 2; pull pillars and great stones). Named for
     the Questlords, NOT the Gauntlet of Fire.
  4. CINDERHOLD (fire region + dungeon): Serielye's old volcano, where the
     Gauntlet of Fire was first kept. Firebuns rekindles his power here.
     Item: EMBER ROD. Serielye is present as an echo (the goddess of balance,
     watching, neither friend nor foe). The players who remember Saga I are
     meant to fear her a little.
  5. BLACK WATER RIDGE (region): a dark ridge of black waterfalls and
     undead camps where the undead are strongest. RIDGE KEEP (dungeon),
     Mara's fortress. Item: IRON FLAIL (breaks undead shields and barred
     gates). One-on-one with MARA at the top of the falls (lore: "a fierce
     one-on-one battle at Black Water Ridge"). With Mara gone, the undead
     army crumbles.
  6. Archmagus Velloran's end: the Order's last stand at the Sanctum's ruin
     in the Fells. Lothelye gives LOTHELYE'S DREAM (the 4th spell) at her
     spring, which lets the hero walk on dream-light and see the astral way.
     The cracked Astral Seal opens the way in.
  7. FINAL DUNGEON: THE ASTRAL RIFT, the biggest in the game (40+ rooms):
     the broken edge of the Astral Dimension, with pieces of every place the
     hero has been floating in starless dark, and the ice prison shattered
     at its heart.
     AVALON'S SCENE (user request, beta 1.3.1): deep in the Astral Dimension,
     before the heart of the Rift, a cutscene with AVALON (Val), Warrior of
     the Cosmos, Keeper of the Stars and Keeper of Hope, born from the
     prayers of Bunsriel's people, Lothelye's kindred ally, bearer of the
     Luminous Blade (Cosmolite, forged in Ladhellin, starlight from
     Lothelye). Her look: overwhelming beauty, short blue hair, brown eyes,
     a beautiful white dress. Her manner: deeply kind and sweet; the one
     warm, gentle moment in the starless dark before the last battle.
     Her theme: avalon.mid (written 1.3.1, _giggle64_work/tools/
     make_avalon_midi.py), A major / D Lydian, on the user's motif sheet
     (giggle-64/avalon motif.png); her second motif is the Pachelbel Canon
     progression (A E F#m C#m D A D E), and the two are sung together at
     the climax. Scene details (what she gives or says) to be decided
     when Chapter V's finale is built.
  8. THE TRUE FINAL BATTLE: Firebuns III, everything he has, three phases
     (flame, Gauntlet, and Denangdyn's shadow through him). Lore: "with one
     final blow from his sword, he struck Firebuns III down and destroyed
     him once and for all."
  9. The remnant of Denangdyn reaches out of the dying Firebuns for the hero.
     SERIELYE appears, as she did for Gigglebuns I (Saga I), and snuffs it
     out without effort: balance restored. She looks at the hero for a long
     moment, and this hero does NOT raise his sword (the opposite of
     Gigglebuns I). She leaves. That ends the Order and Denangdyn thread.
 10. Ending and CREDITS (bittersweet, Super Mario 64 style): a slow tour of
     every region at peace while the credits roll. Haven celebrates; Ashwick
     is rebuilt in the Fells; a candle for Firebuns II; Idrith and Aladriel
     back to back on a dune; Mira on her roof deck; the Bun Sword back on
     its pedestal, its glow fading. Post-credits hook toward Saga V, never
     resolved: dark riders gathering in the Batela Fells, and a scythe.
