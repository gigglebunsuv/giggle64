# Audio credits

Giggle 64's sound effects are recordings shared under Creative Commons licences,
edited (trimmed, layered, pitched, filtered, leveled) for the game by
`_giggle64_work/sfx_swap/build.py`. CC BY sounds are credited below as their
licences require; CC0 sounds are listed too, as thanks.

The musical jingles are MIDI files in `_giggle64_work/sfx_swap/jingles/`, rendered with
Xadra's LoZ Soundfont (2023). That soundfont is built from Nintendo instrument samples,
so the jingles are not covered by a Creative Commons licence.
